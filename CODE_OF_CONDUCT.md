# Community conduct

Everyone is welcome, regardless of background or experience. Be respectful,
explain technical disagreements with evidence, and respect others' time and
privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing another
person's credentials or private diagnostics are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive material or restrict participation to protect
contributors. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer
through their public profile. Reports are handled with discretion; do not
retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.
